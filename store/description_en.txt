BMXt is a keyboard-first command UI for managing tabs, tab groups, and windows in your browser.

Have you ever experienced any of the following?

Mouse operation feels cumbersome
You keep tabs open without meaning to
Finding or organizing the tab you need is hard

When that happens, try classic command-line style operation.

◆What you can do

・Use the prompt to list, search, group, move, reload, and close tabs, and more
・Search history, bookmarks, open page text, and other browser data
・Optional local sync between profiles you connect on the same PC
・Japanese / English UI locale and appearance settings

The above is only part of what this extension can do.
Development continues, with ongoing review and addition of features.

◆Privacy
The BMXt extension processes data entirely on your PC.
The developer does not collect your data or send information to servers operated for profiling.
See the privacy policy for details.

◆Getting started
Launch with the `launch-bmxt` shortcut (default Shift+Alt+C), then try `tutorial` or `tab -list | browse`.
